From research findings to measurable impact

Our observational research examines associations between reported patient experience and subsequent healthcare utilization. We are interested in validating these findings, exploring responsible decision-support applications, and testing interventions that improve access, quality, equity, and appropriate resource use. Observed associations do not establish causation, and proposed applications require prospective evaluation.

Research partnerships

Validate findings across care settings and populations; examine additional clinical, access, and equity factors.

Pilot interventions

Explore targeted care navigation, access, follow-up, and preventive-care support, with measurable outcomes.
