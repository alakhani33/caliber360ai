What changes?

From population insights to measurable value

Repeatedly combining patient experience with existing payer information could help teams identify access and coordination barriers, test supportive interventions, and assess whether they improve appropriate care, equity, utilization, and cost efficiency. Impact must be measured through prospective evaluation; this demonstration does not estimate waste or savings.

How this concept could be evaluated

Start with an eligible, consent-appropriate population. Compare experience-informed outreach against usual workflows. Track access and follow-up, clinically appropriate preventive care, potentially avoidable ED use using validated definitions, member outcomes, disparities, and the full cost of intervention. Examine survey-response bias and whether NPS adds predictive value beyond claims and clinical variables.
