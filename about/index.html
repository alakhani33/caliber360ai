Dr. Ali Lakhani

Founder & Principal Engineer, CALIBER360 AI Solutions

About

Dr. Ali Lakhani is the founder of CALIBER360 AI Solutions, bringing decades of experience in engineering, technology leadership, data science, and organizational strategy. His work connects analytical rigor with practical business understanding to help organizations make more informed decisions.

Dr. Lakhani's career includes experience at Intel, Apollo Group, Sutter Health, and UC Davis Health. At Intel, he worked in technology and organizational leadership. His subsequent work in healthcare data science has focused on applying advanced analytics to consequential operational and strategic questions, including predictive modeling, patient experience, healthcare utilization, and market intelligence.

His technical interests and experience span machine learning, natural language processing, large language models, and emerging AI applications. He places particular emphasis on sound analytical methods, careful evaluation of evidence, and translating complex findings into insights that leaders and practitioners can use.

In addition to his consulting and analytical work, Dr. Lakhani is an educator who has designed and delivered data science and AI boot camps for analysts, engineers, managers, and executives. Through CALIBER360, he combines applied solutions with training that helps organizations develop their own data and AI capabilities. While healthcare is a core area of expertise, his approach is relevant across industries.

Dr. Lakhani holds a doctorate in Organizational Leadership, an MBA, a master's degree in Electrical Engineering, and an undergraduate degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering.

Better questions. Clearer evidence. Smarter decisions.
